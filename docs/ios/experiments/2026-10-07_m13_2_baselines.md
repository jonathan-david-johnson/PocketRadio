## Cached-start blocker

A fresh-context Sol review found no fake-input-controlled happens-before relationship between artwork completion and the initial full rebuild:

- Tracklist prefetch follows episode load.
- `DefaultPlayer.play()` invokes its preparation callback without waiting for ICY buffering. Holding stream audio therefore cannot hold that rebuild.
- Warming the image cache removes a download but leaves independent callback/audio-session scheduling. It increases probability, not determinism.

A deterministic regression scenario needs a separately approved test-only barrier that holds the initial preparation/full-rebuild continuation until the real artwork path has published red. Release then exercises the real rebuild. Barrier setup/timeouts must remain ordinary failures. The review did not implement or experimentally validate such a barrier.

Do not mark the current intermittent scenario as a strict expected failure: its present passing outcome would intermittently fail the suite as an unexpected pass. Do not substitute pause/resume, write the expected output from a helper, or silently defer an approved scenario.

## Why record the output-route category?

It is qualitative provenance for the audible path: speaker, Bluetooth, AirPlay, and other routes can have different buffering or device-side latency, and a route change can invalidate comparisons between the AVPlayer media clock and what a person heard. The category is **not** a measured latency value or a per-route correction to `+160s`. The listener says all current runs used speakers, so route differences do not explain their varying button delays. The in-app category is still useful in each exported session, but a missing category is not a reason to discard a listener report: preserve `unrecorded` in that sidecar and document a retrospective speaker confirmation separately. Do not capture device identifiers.
